# Ayurveda Treatment Reference

A single-page reference for classical Ayurvedic treatment, traced to the book each medicine comes from.

- 51 conditions: symptoms, warning signs, medicines with dose, timing, vehicle and duration, side effects, diet, yoga, evidence rating and what modern medicine advises
- Encyclopedia A–Z: herbs, medicines, terms, yoga practices and 44 classical texts
- Find my treatment guide, daily routine planner, trusted centres, everyday ailments
- Study corner for BAMS students: Samprapti Ghataka notes, quizzes and flashcards
- Search in English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Malayalam and Kannada

## Run it

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step and no dependencies (fonts load from Google Fonts).

## Sources

Ayurvedic Standard Treatment Guidelines (Ministry of AYUSH, 2017); Ayurvedic Formulary of India Parts I–II (via Wellcome Collection, CC BY 4.0); the classical Samhitas and Nighantus. See the Sources and Editorial standards pages in the site.

## Disclaimer

Educational reference, not a prescription. Take medicines only under a qualified Ayurveda doctor (BAMS/MD), never stop prescribed medicines, and get urgent care for any warning sign.
